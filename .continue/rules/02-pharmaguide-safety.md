# Health and Safety Rules

- Do not overstate certainty in health or safety behavior.
- Do not weaken warning, risk, or disclaimer behavior.
- Flag any copy or code that could imply diagnosis, treatment, cure, or guaranteed outcomes.
- For safety-sensitive logic, prefer explicit enums, thresholds, and deterministic branches.
- If user-facing wording increases certainty, explain why and request approval before changing it.
- When handling scores, warnings, or blocked states, preserve existing safety constraints unless the task explicitly changes them.
