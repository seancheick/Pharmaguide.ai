## What this copy is doing

* **First sentence** names the four primary capabilities (safety,
  interactions, ingredients, personalization) without claiming
  diagnosis or medical advice.
* **Second sentence** anchors the differentiator — instant scan,
  multi-axis evaluation, fit to YOUR profile — and closes with the
  brand tone words: **calm, transparent, easy-to-understand**.
* **Avoided phrasing** (these would slide tone or risk regulatory
  flags): "doctor-approved", "diagnose", "prescribe",
  "treatment", "cure", "guarantee", "100%", anything with a
  performance claim.

Keep future revisions of this copy in the same register. If you
want to test a variant, add it as a separate section below — don't
overwrite the canonical block above without bumping the version.
