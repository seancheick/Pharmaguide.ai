## Design Context

### Users

PharmaGuide serves everyday supplement consumers who may be scanning products in a pharmacy, reviewing a submitted product later, or checking a saved stack at home. They need to identify products quickly, understand quality and personal-fit cautions without clinical training, and recover from incomplete photos or rejected submissions without remembering UPCs.

### Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and precise. The product should feel clinically responsible without becoming cold or intimidating, and polished enough to inspire confidence in health-related decisions.

### Aesthetic Direction

A refined, native-feeling iOS interface with strong information hierarchy, restrained color, readable evidence and safety states, and consistent light and dark themes. Prefer clear product names, concise explanations, and purposeful progressive disclosure. Avoid dense technical dashboards, generic card grids, decorative effects, and UI that makes users interpret database identifiers.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision a person needs: product identity, score, safety state, and next action.
2. Explain evidence and uncertainty in plain language without overstating clinical certainty.
3. Keep critical actions obvious, responsive, accessible, and usable with the keyboard visible.
4. Use one canonical data meaning across pipeline, catalog, and app; presentation must not recalculate clinical conclusions.
5. Make edge cases recoverable: preserve user work, name products visibly, and provide direct retry or correction paths.
